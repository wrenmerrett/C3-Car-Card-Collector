HOW TO PLAY

    Click the Collect button to collect a new card. Duplicates will give you bonus cash.
    Enter the Collection and click on the colour-coded Rarity tabs to view the cars you've collected
    In Collection, you can change the cars in your hand by removing them from the hand display at the top by clicking them, and adding by clicking on cards in the Owned tab.
    Boost the stats of your hand to increase your collecting power - top speed increases your pull luck, 0-60 time decreases the button cooldown, and handling improves your earnings.
    Equip cards with the same attributes, then hit the Activate Synergies switch to activate synergy bonuses and get a chance to focus in on the cars you want.
    Buy Elite cars to unlock powerful Elite Perks and upgrade them with Elite Tools, as well as using Elite Tools to install Elite Perks on any car you like!
    Complete Missions and Milestones to unlock cash, Stars, and exclusive Elite cars.
    Use the Save and Load buttons to keep your progress! Make sure to only Load at the start of a new session.
