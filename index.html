How to Play

Welcome to C3 - the Car Card Collector.



This is a card. You start with the 5 basic ones you see in the Play tab, and can collect more by pressing the COLLECT button.



These cars go to your Collection tab, where you can filter them by their rarity and many other attributes.



Pressing on a card in your Current Hand will remove it, allowing you to add a new car from your Collection.



Improving your hand is key to collecting more than just basic cars.


- TOP SPEED improves your LUCK, allowing you to collect higher rarity cars.


- 0-60 TIME improves your COOLDOWN, allowing you to collect cars more often.


- HANDLING improves your EARNINGS, allowing you to earn more money from collecting.


- Multtiples of other car attributes allow you to trigger SYNERGIES with the ACTIVATE SYNERGIES button - these give you a higher chance of collecting cars with those attributes.

Further Progression

The Dealership tab allows you to purchase specific cars from a selection. You must also refresh the dealership once - first one's free - to activate the Save button.



It also allows you to PRESTIGE cars you already own by buying duplicates, to bank Prestige Coins.



The Dealership is also your first and easiest source of Elite cars, and can be upgraded to make more available.



Elite cars come with special perks that improve your performance. Descriptions of these perks can be found in the Elite tab, where they can also be upgraded.



You will need Elite Tools for these upgrades, which are earned passively by collecting once you have some Elite cars.



Acquiring Elite cars also unlocks Heat levels in the Play tab. These allow you to impose an RQ limit on your hand in return for boosts to the 3 main stats.

Contracts, Milestones and Prestige

Contracts, Milestones and Prestige give you even more ways to earn Elite cars.


- CONTRACTS reward Stars, which can be used to purchase exclusive Elite cars from the Star Shop.


- MILESTONES are permanent progression unlocks that will reward persistent players with very strong Elites.


- PRESTIGE allows you to reset your garage and cash to bank eanred Prestige Coins, which can be used to permanently unlock any non-Milestone Elite car.



Use these features to amp your late-game progression!
